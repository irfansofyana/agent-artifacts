01 · Strategy

Your shortest credible path.

Because you already work with AWS and GenAI, four weeks at 6–8 focused hours per week is realistic. The trap is over-studying LLM engineering while under-studying AWS service selection, governance, and exam wording.

The pass strategy:

Put 52% of study time into Domains 2 and 3, then deliberately protect the 28% from responsible AI, security, compliance, and governance. Use practice questions to train “best AWS answer” judgment.

Domain 1 · Fundamentals of AI and ML — 20%
Master
  • AI vs ML vs deep learning vs GenAI vs agentic AI
  • Supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning
  • Regression, classification, and clustering use cases
  • Batch, real-time, asynchronous, and serverless inference

Decision patterns
  • Deterministic outcome required → conventional software, not predictive ML
  • Fraud flag → classification; demand amount → regression; customer segments → clustering
  • Speech to text → Transcribe; entities/sentiment → Comprehend; chatbot → Lex

Domain 3 · Applications of Foundation Models — 28%
Master
  • FM selection: modality, context, multilingual, latency, size, price, prompt caching
  • Temperature and input/output length trade-offs
  • RAG, vector stores, Bedrock Knowledge Bases, AI agents
  • Zero-, single-, and few-shot prompting; templates; negative prompts
  • Pre-training, continuous pre-training, fine-tuning, distillation, RLHF
High-yield decisions
  • Fresh/private facts with citations → RAG
  • Behavior/style/format specialization → fine-tuning
  • Fast and cheap behavior guidance → prompting or in-context examples
  • Smaller/faster model preserving teacher behavior → distillation
  • Evaluate with task metrics plus business outcomes, not benchmark scores alone

03 · Retrieval

Flashcards that teach decisions.

Say the answer aloud before revealing it. These emphasize high-confusion comparisons and current-scope concepts.

Card 1

Recall protocol

1. Answer without peeking.
2. Explain the AWS service cue.
3. Name the tempting wrong option.
4. Cycle until every answer feels obvious.

Exam discipline: no penalty for guessing. Answer every question, flag uncertainty, and return later.

06 · Scheduling

Turn preparation into an appointment.

Book when the date will focus you—not after you feel perfectly ready. For your profile, a target 4–5 weeks out is sensible.

Create or open your AWS Certification Account

This is separate from a normal AWS account and uses AWS Builder ID. Make your profile name match your government ID.

Request ESL +30 before scheduling, if you want it

AWS offers a 30-minute extension to non-native English speakers taking an English exam. In the account, choose Request Exam Accommodations → Request Accommodation → ESL +30 MINUTES. Do this before booking.

Select Schedule New Exam

Find AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01), choose Schedule with Pearson VUE, then select online proctoring or a test center.

Choose language, place, date, and time

English online proctoring is generally available 24/7. A localized exam lets you toggle to English questions. Testing-center inventory varies.

Pay and read the confirmation

The listed base price is USD 100; tax and currency conversion can vary. Pearson VUE accepts supported cards or vouchers. The confirmation email is operationally important—read all of it.

Prepare the exact test setup

For online testing, run OnVUE’s system test on the same computer, network, and room. Check in up to 30 minutes early. More than 15 minutes late can forfeit the appointment and fee.

Readiness gate

Policy details worth remembering
You can reschedule up to 24 hours before the appointment, at most twice. Inside 24 hours, cancellation/rescheduling is unavailable and the fee is normally forfeited.

Schedule for a quiet Saturday morning, request ESL +30 first if useful, and prefer a testing center if your home connection/room cannot be made boringly reliable. Online is convenient; operational uncertainty is not.
